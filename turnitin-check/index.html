Turnitin Report Forensics

拖入一份或多份 Turnitin AI Writing Report PDF，检查报告时间链、PDF 元数据、Submission ID、页数、字体和内部对象结构。所有分析都在浏览器本地完成。

Important: 这个工具只能识别“技术一致性/异常”，不能仅凭 PDF 证明报告一定真实或一定伪造。最强验证方式仍然是查看 Turnitin 在线 submission 页面。
🔒 Files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
Fingerprint comparison
Heuristic reference rules are based on recurring patterns seen in genuine-looking Turnitin exports. Different LMS integrations, report versions and export paths may legitimately produce different PDFs. Treat the result as forensic triage, not a final authenticity verdict.
